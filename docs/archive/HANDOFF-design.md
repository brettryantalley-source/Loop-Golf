# Design brief — Loop Golf (ghost match only)

Paste this whole file into a Claude Design thread. Attach one screenshot each of the current
mid-round and setup screens for content reference only. Nothing else is needed.

---

Design phone screens (375×812, portrait) for **Loop Golf**, a golf side game one person plays
against a "ghost": an opponent projected from the player's own recent form onto the course being
played. Used outdoors in sunlight, one-handed, often with no signal. Big legible numbers,
thumb-reachable controls, nothing that depends on network.

**The game.** 18 holes. The ghost's score on every hole is fixed before the round. You log one
score per hole. Six 3-hole segments (1 point, ½ for a tie), front nine (½), back nine (½), total (1):
8 points. The feeling: a head-to-head match against a silent, ever-present opponent — competitive,
a little haunted, satisfying to track hole by hole.

**Real content — use this, no lorem:**
- Course: Sugar Creek Golf Club, Blue tee, 70.1/125, par 71. The ghost plays to 78.
- Now on hole 7, par 4, stroke index 5. The ghost makes 4 here.
- Match: YOU 2.5 · GHOST 0.5 → "1 UP". Segments: S1 won 14–15, S2 won 14–16, S3–S6 not played.
  Front nine leading; back nine and total open.
- Holes so far, you vs ghost: 1 tie, 2 loss, 3 win, 4 win, 5 win, 6 win.
- Record vs the ghost: 4–2–1, streak W2, average margin +1.4. Last-5 differential: 6.9.

**Screens.** Design 1 and 2; add 3 if it helps sell a direction.
1. **Mid-round (the hero).** Current hole unmistakable at a glance. Log a score fast — one tap for
   par is the goal. Jump to any hole. See how the match stands: points, the six segments, front /
   back / total, and per-hole results. A way to finish the round once all 18 are in.
2. **Setup.** Pick course and tee, see the ghost's projected score and your record, confirm the
   differential, start.
3. **Result.** Did you beat the ghost, the point breakdown, the scorecard.

**Not in scope:** anything about clubs, aiming, GPS, maps or satellite. This is the match only.

**Constraints:** portrait only; no webfonts or remote assets; the numbers above are the content,
the layout and look are yours. There is no brand to follow.

**Deliverable:** three distinct directions for the mid-round screen — different layout, type and
feel, so choosing is a real decision — then the other screens in the chosen direction.
